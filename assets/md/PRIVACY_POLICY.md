# Privacy Policy for Fire Todo
**Last Updated: December 2025**

At **Fire Todo**, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide our services, we collect the following information:

* **Account Data**: Email address and password used for **Sign Up** and **Login**.
* **Verification Data**: Email address used for **Email Verification**.
* **User Content**: Tasks (Todos) that you **Create**, **Edit**, or **Delete**.
* **Profile Information**: Any data you provide in your **Profile** section.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your data for the following purposes:

* To maintain your account and ensure its security.
* To synchronize your tasks across all your devices.
* To provide the core functionality of managing your todos.

## 3. Data Control
You have full control over your information:

* **Edit/Delete**: You can modify or remove individual tasks at any time.
* **Delete All**: You can use this feature to permanently clear all your stored tasks from our database.

## 4. Security
We implement standard security measures to protect your information, but we encourage you to always use a strong and unique password for your account.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the **Fire Todo** development team.
