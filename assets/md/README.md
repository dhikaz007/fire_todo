# Fire Todo 🔥

**Fire Todo** is a clean, fast, and secure task management application designed to help you organize your daily life with ease. Built with Flutter and Firebase, it ensures your data is always synced and accessible.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Secure Authentication**: 
    * Easy **Sign Up** and **Login** process.
    * **Email Verification** to ensure account security.
* **Complete Task Management**: 
    * **Create** new tasks instantly.
    * **Edit** existing tasks to stay updated.
    * **Delete** completed or unwanted tasks.
* **Data Control**: 
    * **Delete All** feature to clear your entire list in one click.
* **User Profile**: 
    * Manage your personal information in the **Profile** section.
* **Real-time Sync**: 
    * Keep your todos synchronized across all your devices automatically.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend**: [Flutter](https://flutter.dev) (Dart)
* **Backend**: [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com) (Auth & Firestore)
* **State Management**: Provider / Bloc / Riverpod (pilih salah satu)
